# Sound library provenance

All clips in `public/sounds/` were generated locally with **Stable Audio 3 Small-SFX** (`model_id: small-sfx`) via the Sound Studio helper (`C:\Projekte\SoundStudio`, `POST /generate`), then post-processed with numpy/soundfile:

- Short one-shots were generated at 3 s (the model degrades into clipped noise below ~2 s), 2-3 candidates per requested variation; the best candidates were picked numerically (crest factor, decay, no clipping) and cut around the onset/transient.
- One-shots: downmixed to mono, DC removed, leading silence trimmed (5 ms pre-roll), cut to target length, cosine fade-out, peak-normalized to about -1 dBFS.
- Loops (`amb_*`, `car_idle`): equal-power crossfade of the tail into the head (2 s; 0.75 s for `car_idle`), so the end flows sample-continuously into the start. Ambience stays stereo, slow gain-riding (2 s window, max +/-9 dB) evens out the level, RMS normalized to about -24 dBFS (`car_idle`: mono, -18 dBFS).
- 44.1 kHz, Ogg Vorbis (libsndfile). Suggested playback gains are in `manifest.json`.
