Norgo animation clips
=====================

clips.bin and clips.json in this folder are derived (resampled to 30 fps, reduced to the body
bones, converted to model-space rotations) by tools/build-anim-clips.ts from the

  Quaternius Universal Animation Library (Standard), glTF edition
  https://quaternius.com — https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library

released by Quaternius under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Thank you, Quaternius. The derived data is likewise offered under CC0.


Motion-captured clips (CMU_*)
-----------------------------

The clips named CMU_* (the standing idles) are derived by tools/cmu-bvh.ts (retargeted onto the
body bones above, cut, looped, resampled to 30 fps) from takes of the

  CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database — http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu
  The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
  The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

  CMU_Idle_1, CMU_Idle_2 (mirrored): subject 113, trial 21 ("Standing Still")
  CMU_Idle_3:                         subject 77, trial 2 ("standing")

in the BVH conversion by B. Hahne (cgspeed.com; mirrored at https://github.com/Shriinivas/cmubvh).
CMU places no restrictions on the use of the data (commercial use included; see the FAQ at
mocap.cs.cmu.edu), and the BVH conversion adds none. Thank you, Carnegie Mellon University.
