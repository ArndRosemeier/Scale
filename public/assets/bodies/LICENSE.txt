Built-in characters
===================

woman.glb is the base female model Arnd Rosemeier found on Blend Swap (converted with
tools/avatar), with skin, hair and eye textures painted by tools/avatar/base-bodies/.
man.glb is derived from the same mesh, skeleton and UVs by tools/avatar/base-bodies/
(reshaped and textured there). woman.png / man.png are their picker thumbnails.

Source model, author and licence on Blend Swap: to be filled in.
